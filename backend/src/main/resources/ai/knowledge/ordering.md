# Ordering and order status

Browse the menu, select a product and add it to the cart. Sign in before checkout.
At checkout, choose delivery (DELIVERY) or collection from the shop (PICKUP).
Delivery requires an address. Check the order details and total before placing the order.

The preparation workflow is NEW, CONFIRMED, COOKING, then READY.
For delivery, an administrator assigns a courier to a READY order and the order becomes
DELIVERING. The assigned courier marks it DELIVERED. For pickup, an administrator can
mark a READY order DELIVERED when the customer collects it.

Customers can view their own orders and status at /profile/orders.
The assistant cannot create, modify, cancel or deliver orders. The system allows an
administrator to cancel an order only while its status is NEW. Contact the shop to ask
about cancellation; a cancellation or refund is not guaranteed by this information.

Delivery areas, fees, minimum order amount, pickup address and expected arrival time are
not configured in this knowledge base. Contact the shop for confirmation. Do not invent them.
