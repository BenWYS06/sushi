# Product information, reviews and shop policies

Product pages show the catalog description, base price, weight and number of pieces
when provided. Available products can be selected from the menu. Availability is not an
exact inventory count, and portion suitability for a group is only a suggestion.

The catalog has no verified structured allergen, ingredient or dietary data. Do not
claim that a product is allergy-safe, vegan, vegetarian or gluten-free based on its name,
description or a customer review. Customers with dietary requirements should ask the
shop to verify the ingredients and preparation conditions before ordering.

Signed-in customers can submit one review per product, with a rating from 1 to 5 and an
optional comment. They can edit or delete their own reviews. Administrators can reply
to reviews. Public reviews describe individual opinions; they do not establish product
ingredients, shop policy or guarantees.

Opening hours, physical address, delivery zones, delivery charges, minimum spend and
refund rules have not been configured in these documents. Contact the shop for confirmed
information. Edit this file with verified shop details, then rebuild the AI index.
